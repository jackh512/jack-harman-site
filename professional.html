At Harman Corp, a manufacturer of protective caps, plugs, grips, and molded plastic components, I grow the business by identifying new markets, building customer relationships, and turning research into sales opportunities. The role

From research to repeat customers.

A major part of my work involves finding manufacturers whose products already use components Harman can supply. I research industries, analyze product catalogs and photographs, and identify specific applications for our products. This allows me to approach prospective customers with a clear understanding of their needs and opportunities to improve pricing, quality, or supply.

I also move opportunities through the sales process, from initial outreach and sample requests to gathering specifications, preparing quotes, following up, and developing ongoing customer relationships. Throughout that process, I communicate Harman's manufacturing capabilities and help customers understand how our products fit their applications.
